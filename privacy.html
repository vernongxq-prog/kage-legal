隐私政策

本隐私政策说明 tepgu consulting K.K.（以下简称"我们"）如何收集、使用和保护您在使用 ペラペラ日本語（Kage Japanese Shadowing）（以下简称"本应用"）时的个人信息。我们尊重您的隐私，仅收集运行本应用所必需的最少信息。

1. 我们是谁

本应用由 tepgu consulting K.K. 开发和运营，公司注册于日本。如有任何隐私相关问题，请通过以下方式联系：KKousen@tepguconsulting.com

2. 我们收集的信息

2.1 您主动提供的内容

  • 学习文本：您在"影子跟读"和"AI 情景对话"功能中输入的日语文本和会话内容
  • 录音文件：您在跟读练习中录制的音频（用于本地回放和可选的 AI 发音评分）
  • 查询关键词：您在单词查询中点击的日语单词
  • 个人资料（可选）：昵称、性别、母语偏好、日语水平等您主动填写的内容
  • 邮箱（可选）：当您选择从匿名账户升级到注册账户时，我们会收集您提供的邮箱地址用于账号识别和恢复

2.2 自动收集的信息

  • 匿名用户 ID：首次启动时，Supabase 自动为您分配一个匿名 UUID，作为您的账号标识。该 ID 不与任何个人身份信息绑定
  • 使用配额计数：免费版每日 / 每月的功能使用次数（用于配额管理，不含具体内容）
  • 订阅状态：通过 Apple 应用内购买（IAP）处理的订阅信息（订单凭证、订阅级别、订阅周期）

2.3 您的账户中保存的内容

以下数据保存在您的 Supabase 账户中，与您的匿名 UUID 关联，仅您本人可访问（通过应用层 RLS 策略隔离）：

  • 单词本
  • 跟读练习记录及录音文件
  • AI 对话历史
  • 个人资料和应用设置

您卸载应用不会自动删除云端数据。如需彻底删除，请通过应用内"删除账户"功能或邮件联系我们。

2.4 我们不收集的信息

我们不会收集您的真实姓名、电话号码、位置数据、联系人、相册、健康数据、广告标识符（IDFA）或任何其他用于跨应用追踪的个人识别信息。

3. 我们如何使用信息

  • 提供本应用的核心功能（文本优化、语音朗读、对话点评、发音评分、单词查询）
  • 在您的设备和云账户之间同步学习数据
  • 处理订阅购买和恢复
  • 管理免费版使用配额
  • 统计聚合使用数据以改善服务（不含个人识别信息）

4. 第三方服务

本应用使用以下第三方服务，您输入的数据可能会传输给它们：

4.1 Supabase（后端服务，主要数据处理方）

本应用使用 Supabase 作为后端数据库、用户认证和文件存储服务。您的所有学习数据（单词本、跟读历史、对话记录、录音文件、个人资料）都存储在 Supabase 上。Supabase 的隐私政策：https://supabase.com/privacy

4.2 Google（Gemini AI）

您在 AI 情景对话中输入的内容、以及对话评估请求会发送至 Google Gemini API 进行处理。Google 不会使用这些数据训练通用模型（基于 Google AI for Developers 的 API 服务条款）。Google 隐私政策：https://policies.google.com/privacy

4.3 Microsoft Azure（Pro 用户）

仅当您使用 Pro 高级语音播报或 AI 发音评分功能时，相应的文本或录音会发送至 Microsoft Azure Speech Service 进行处理。Microsoft 隐私声明：https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement

4.4 Jisho.org（公开词典）

当您点击查询单词时，单词会发送到 Jisho.org 的公开 API 获取释义。Jisho.org 是一个免费日语词典服务（基于 JMdict 项目）。

4.5 Apple（应用内购买）

所有订阅交易由 Apple 处理。我们不会接触您的支付信息。详见 Apple 的隐私政策：https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

4.6 RevenueCat（订阅状态管理）

我们使用 RevenueCat 验证和管理订阅状态。RevenueCat 仅接收 Apple 订单凭证和您的匿名 UUID。详见：https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/

5. 数据保留与删除

云端数据保留在 Supabase 上，直到您主动删除或注销账户。卸载本应用不会自动删除云端数据。

如需彻底删除您的账户和所有数据，请通过应用内"删除账户"功能或发送邮件至 KKousen@tepguconsulting.com 申请删除，我们会在 30 天内处理。

第三方服务的数据保留按其各自的政策执行。

  • 删除：您可以在应用内删除任何单条记录，或申请注销账户彻底清除全部数据
  • 取消订阅：通过 iOS 设置 → Apple ID → 订阅 进行管理

如果您是 EU/EEA 居民，您还享有 GDPR 规定的访问、更正、删除、限制处理、数据可携带权和反对处理等权利。请联系我们行使这些权利。

所有网络传输均使用 HTTPS 加密。我们采取合理的技术和管理措施保护您的数据，但请理解互联网传输无法保证 100% 安全。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how tepgu consulting K.K. ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use ペラペラ日本語 (Kage Japanese Shadowing) ("the App"). We respect your privacy and collect only the minimum information necessary to operate the App.

1. Who We Are

The App is developed and operated by tepgu consulting K.K., a company registered in Japan. For privacy-related inquiries, contact us at: KKousen@tepguconsulting.com

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Learning content: Japanese text and dialogue you enter in the "Shadowing Practice" and "AI Conversation" features
  • Audio recordings: Recordings made during shadowing practice (used for playback and optional AI pronunciation scoring)
  • Lookup queries: Japanese words you tap to look up
  • Profile (optional): Nickname, gender, native language preference, JLPT level — only if you choose to fill them in
  • Email (optional): If you choose to upgrade from an anonymous account to a registered account, we collect the email address you provide for account identification and recovery

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Anonymous user ID: On first launch, Supabase automatically assigns you an anonymous UUID as your account identifier. This ID is not linked to any personally identifiable information
  • Usage counters: Daily and monthly counts of feature usage in the free tier (count only, no content)
  • Subscription status: Subscription details processed via Apple In-App Purchase (IAP) — receipts, plan type, dates

2.3 Information Stored in Your Account

The following data is stored on Supabase, associated with your anonymous UUID, and accessible only to you (isolated by row-level security policies at the application layer):

  • Your saved vocabulary
  • Shadowing history and audio recordings
  • AI dialogue history
  • Profile and app settings

Uninstalling the App does not automatically delete cloud data. To fully delete your data, use the in-app "Delete Account" function or contact us by email.

2.4 Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect your real name, phone number, location, contacts, photos, health data, advertising identifiers (IDFA), or any other personally identifiable information used for cross-app tracking.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide the App's core features (text optimization, speech, dialogue feedback, pronunciation scoring, vocabulary lookup)
  • To sync your learning data between device and cloud account
  • To process subscription purchases and restoration
  • To enforce free-tier usage quotas
  • To improve the service based on aggregated, non-identifying usage analytics

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services. Data you provide may be transmitted to them:

4.1 Supabase (Backend / Primary Data Processor)

The App uses Supabase as its backend database, authentication provider, and file storage. All your learning data (vocabulary, shadowing history, dialogue history, audio recordings, profile) is stored on Supabase. Supabase Privacy Policy: https://supabase.com/privacy

4.2 Google (Gemini AI)

Content you enter in AI scenario conversations and conversation evaluation requests is sent to the Google Gemini API for processing. Per the Google AI for Developers API Service Terms, Google does not use this data to train its general-purpose models. Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4.3 Microsoft Azure (Pro users only)

When you use the Pro premium voice or AI pronunciation scoring features, the corresponding text or recording is sent to Microsoft Azure Speech Service for processing. Microsoft Privacy Statement: https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement

4.4 Jisho.org (Public Dictionary)

When you tap a word to look up its definition, the word is sent to the public Jisho.org API. Jisho.org is a free Japanese dictionary service based on the JMdict project.

4.5 Apple (In-App Purchases)

All subscription transactions are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

4.6 RevenueCat (Subscription State Management)

We use RevenueCat to validate and manage subscription state. RevenueCat receives only Apple receipts and your anonymous UUID. Details: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/

Cloud data is retained on Supabase until you actively delete it or close your account. Uninstalling the App does not automatically delete cloud data.

To fully delete your account and all data, use the in-app "Delete Account" function, or email KKousen@tepguconsulting.com — we will process the request within 30 days.

Data retention by third-party services is governed by their respective policies.

  • Cancel subscription: Manage via iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions

If you are an EU/EEA resident, you also have rights under GDPR including access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and objection. Contact us to exercise these rights.

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you discover that a child has provided us with information, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Data Security

All network transmissions are encrypted with HTTPS. We employ reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, but please understand that no transmission over the internet is 100% secure.
